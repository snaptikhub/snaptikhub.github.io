How to Download TikTok Videos

The complete guide to saving TikTok videos without a watermark on Android, iPhone and PC — it takes less than 30 seconds.

Step 1 — Copy the TikTok video link

Every download starts with the video's link. In the TikTok app, open the video you want to save, tap the Share arrow on the right side of the screen, scroll the bottom row of options and tap Copy link. On a computer, open the video on tiktok.com and copy the full address from your browser's address bar. Short links from the share sheet (vm.tiktok.com or vt.tiktok.com) work just as well as full links.

Step 2 — Paste the link into SnapTik

Come back to this page and paste the link into the box at the top. On a phone, tap the paste icon inside the box to insert the link from your clipboard in one tap. Then press the Download button. SnapTik fetches the video's details — you will see its cover, title, author and view counts while the download options are prepared.

Step 3 — Choose quality and save

Pick the option that suits you:

  • HD — No Watermark: the sharpest version available, ideal for reposting, editing or watching on a big screen.
  • No Watermark (SD): a smaller file that downloads faster on slow connections.
  • With Watermark: the standard version that keeps the creator's TikTok username on the video.
  • MP3: only the sound of the video, saved as an audio file.

Downloading on iPhone (iOS)

Use Safari for the smoothest experience on iPhone and iPad. After you tap a download button, the video may open in the browser player first — tap the share or download icon in the player and choose Save Video to move it into your Photos app. If a file lands in the Files app instead, open it there, tap Share and select Save Video.

Downloading on Android

In Chrome or any Android browser, the file is saved directly to your Downloads folder and usually appears in your Gallery app within a few seconds. If your browser asks where to save the file, the default Downloads location is the easiest to find later.

Downloading on PC or Mac

On a computer the video is saved to your browser's Downloads folder as a standard MP4 file. It plays in every media player and can be dropped straight into editing software such as CapCut, Premiere Pro or iMovie.

Troubleshooting

  • "Could not fetch this video": the video is probably private, deleted, or region-locked. SnapTik can only download public videos.
  • The link is rejected: make sure the copied text contains a tiktok.com, vm.tiktok.com, vt.tiktok.com or douyin.com link — extra text around the link is fine, SnapTik finds the URL inside it.
  • The video opens instead of downloading: some browsers (especially on iPhone) preview the file. Use the player's share/download button to save it, as described above.
  • Slow fetching: popular videos at busy times can take a few extra seconds. Wait for the result card rather than pressing Download repeatedly.
